01 · Colors

Soft dusty blues, one yellow.

The palette leads with soft blue-white and near-black text. Yellow is the accent - used sparingly on badges, dots, CTAs, and as a highlighter behind key words in headlines. Never as body text.

02 · Typography

Inter Tight. Everything.

One font, one family. Editorial modern grotesk with tight tracking. Distinct from TRUE Original (Marcellus serif + Helvetica Neue) - Upskill leads with sans-serif confidence. No italic for emphasis - use color + yellow highlighter background instead.

05 · Voice & tone

Curated. Concrete. Never hype.

Upskill speaks to the learner, not the educator. Confident but never loud. Everything must be provable - if we say "verified" we mean it can be verified.

Do

Say what it is.

Concrete over abstract. Course names, educator names, real numbers.

  • "Learn today. Prove it forever."
  • "Every course ends with proof."
  • "200+ trusted educators. 8,400+ courses."
  • "Courses worth your time."
Don't

Hype, jargon, or filler.

No superlatives, no urgency tricks, no "in today's world."

  • "Unlock your potential"
  • "World-class learning platform"
  • "Revolutionary credential marketplace"
  • "Discover your future today"
