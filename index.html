The look, sound, and feel of True Upskill.

True Upskill is its own brand on its own domain. This page is the single written source for its colors, type, logo use, components, and tone. Where this page and the live site ever disagree, the site at trueupskill.com is correct and this page gets updated. Never the other way round.

Owner: Patrik Updated: 2026-07-25 Status: v1.0 · reconciled Sources: trueupskill.com + TRUE-Catalog global.css
01 · Brand position

A standalone brand. Not a TRUE product.

True Upskill is a course catalog where every course ends in a credential that can be verified at the source. It lives at trueupskill.com and carries its own name, mark, typeface, and palette.

What it is
  • Its own consumer brand at trueupskill.com, with its own guideline (this page).
  • The wordmark is True Upskill, in exactly that casing. Writing it as TRUE Upskill is wrong. This casing is a deliberate decision by Patrik.
  • Swedish first: the site defaults to Swedish, English lives at /en.
  • The verification technology behind the credentials comes from TRUE. That relationship stays in the background of the product, it does not appear in the brand surface.
What it is not
  • Not a sibling of TRUE Sign, Vault, Verify, or Profile. It is not part of that product family or its navigation.
  • Not set in Marcellus or Helvetica Neue. Those belong to TRUE Original. Upskill is Inter Tight, everything.
  • The mark is not the TRUE gem. It is Upskill's own shield in a rounded tile.
  • The TRUE style guide at style.true.gate1.dev does not govern this brand.

Rule of precedence, set by Patrik 2026-07-25: the live site sets the rules. Values on this page were read from TRUE-Catalog/develop/src/styles/global.css and from the HTML that trueupskill.com actually ships.

02 · Logo

Shield mark + wordmark.

The mark is a white outlined shield forming a T over a U, on a deep navy ground (#111924). It is a raster original: the exact file, never a redrawing. In product it is always rendered as a rounded tile, with corner radius applied in code.

Hard rules
  • Use the actual file. The repo rule on the mark, verbatim intent: do not recreate it as an inline SVG or redraw the paths. That produced the wrong mark before.
  • Do not recolor, mirror, crop into the strokes, or change the shield's proportions.
  • Always pair with the wordmark True Upskill where space allows. The mark may stand alone at favicon sizes.
In practice
  • Nav renders the mark at 32 × 32 px with 8 px radius, the footer at 44 × 44 px with 10 px radius, both with object-fit: cover. Radius sits at roughly a quarter of the rendered size; keep that ratio when scaling.
  • Wordmark: Inter Tight 700, tracking -0.02em. 18 px in the nav, 24 px in the footer. Full values in the table below.
  • Clear space has no formal spec in the code. Recommendation until one exists: keep at least half the mark's width free on all sides, and never crowd the lockup's 10 px gap.

Wordmark, exact values

The wordmark is live text, not an image file. It is set in the site's own typeface, so it inherits every future type change automatically. Do not export it as a PNG or SVG and do not typeset it in any other family.

03 · Color

Soft dusty blues, one yellow.

The palette leads with soft blue white surfaces and near black ink. Yellow is the single accent: badges, dots, CTAs, and the highlighter behind key words. Never body text. On dark surfaces the accent shifts to Signal, a dusty light blue.

Inter Tight. Everything.

07 · Shadow & motion

Barely there, always quick.

Shadows are soft, blue tinted, and rare: resting cards have none, only floating elements and the search pill carry one. Motion is fast and small: most transitions run 0.15 to 0.18 seconds, and hover lifts cards exactly 2 px.

Search pill / hero 0 4px 24px rgba(38,51,61,0.06)
Hover, floating cards 0 8px 32px rgba(38,51,61,0.08)
Dropdown panels 0 12px 40px rgba(30,43,52,0.14)
Hover me · the standard card hover

Border turns ink, the card lifts 2 px: border-color: var(--ink) + transform: translateY(-2px) over all 0.18s.

Timing

Hovers and color changes: 0.15s. Cards and buttons: 0.18s. Dropdown reveal: 0.18s with a 6 px slide. Marquee loop: 40s linear. Nothing bounces, nothing eases longer than 0.3s.

09 · Navigation & footer

Light head, dark foot.

The nav is sticky and translucent: the page background at 92% opacity behind a 20 px blur, with a 1 px border below. The footer is the one large dark surface, ink dark with a huge headline and a yellow accent word.

Navigation spec
  • Sticky, rgba(234,239,242,0.92) + backdrop-filter: blur(20px), bottom border.
  • Lockup left. Links 14px/500. A ghost pill button on the right.
  • Categories open a two column dropdown panel: white, radius 16px, soft shadow.
  • Language switcher: outlined pill with a globe, dropdown menu radius 12px.

11 · Voice & tone

Curated. Concrete. Never hype.

Upskill speaks to the learner, not the educator. Confident but never loud. Everything must be provable: if we say verified, it can be verified. The real numbers, from the site itself: 10 000+ verified courses, 200+ trusted educators, 400 000+ credentials issued.

Do · all lines below ship on the live site

Say what it is.

Concrete over abstract. Course names, educator names, real numbers.

  • "Lär dig idag. Bevisa det för alltid." · "Learn today. Prove it forever."
  • "Riktiga människor. Äkta diplom." · "Real people. Authentic diplomas."
  • "Tre löften." · "Three promises."
  • "200+ betrodda utbildare. 10 000+ verifierade kurser."
Don't

Hype, jargon, or filler.

No superlatives, no urgency tricks, no "in today's world."

  • "Unlock your potential"
  • "World-class learning platform"
  • "Revolutionary credential marketplace"
  • "Discover your future today"
